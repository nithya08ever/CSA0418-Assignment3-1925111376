RAID CAPACITY CALCULATION
Number of disks = 6
Capacity per disk = 4 TB
Raw capacity = 6 x 4 = 24 TB

RAID 0 = 6 x 4 = 24 TB
RAID 1 = 1 x 4 = 4 TB (six-way mirroring assumption)
RAID 5 = (6 - 1) x 4 = 20 TB
RAID 10 = (6 / 2) x 4 = 12 TB
RAID 6 = (6 - 2) x 4 = 16 TB

Recommendation: RAID 10 for a hospital records server when
performance and predictable recovery are priorities. RAID 6
is an alternative when greater usable capacity and two-disk
failure tolerance are more important.
